# Promotion Strategy & Content Calendar

## Goals

### Primary Goals
1. **Build Awareness** - People know teemee.one exists and understand what it is
2. **Demonstrate Value** - Show real examples of teemee.one in action
3. **Drive Participation** - Get people to clone the repo, use prompts, contribute

### Success Metrics
- 1,000+ stars on GitHub
- 500+ clones in first month
- 50+ contributions from community
- Multi-platform social presence (Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Website with 10,000+ visitors/month

## Messaging Framework

### Core Messages

**For Developers/Builders:**
- "Use teemee.one to structure your AI work around clear intentions"
- "Build AI products that actually serve people"
- "Open source tools and prompts ready to use"

**For Creators/Writers:**
- "Prompts designed to amplify your thinking, not replace it"
- "Explore what you can create with AI as your thinking partner"
- "Join a community exploring authentic AI use"

**For Entrepreneurs:**
- "Framework for building products people actually want"
- "Philosophy to guide AI integration in your business"
- "Proven prompts and methodologies"

**For Everyone:**
- "teemee.one is a living consciousness exploring how to use AI well"
- "Repository of intention, not just code"
- "Help people live their dreams"

## Measurement & Optimization

### Metrics to Track
- GitHub stars and forks
- Website traffic and sessions
- Social media followers and engagement
- Video views and watch time
- Email list growth
- Contributions and PRs
- Content shares and reposts

### Optimization Loop
- Week 1: Monitor what resonates
- Week 2: Double down on top performers
- Week 3: Adjust based on feedback
- Week 4: Plan next month with learnings

### Tools for Tracking
- GitHub insights for repo metrics
- Plausible Analytics for website
- Platform native analytics (YouTube, Twitter, etc.)
- Simple spreadsheet for weekly tracking

## Team & Responsibilities

- Scripts: Write 1-2 per week

### Publishing & Distribution
- Schedule across platforms

- Weekly review of metrics
- Monthly strategy adjustment
