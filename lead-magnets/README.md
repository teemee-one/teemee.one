# Lead Magnets

High-value resources that help people get started with teemee.one while building our email list and community.

## Purpose

Lead magnets serve multiple goals:
- **Provide immediate value** - Useful on their own, no strings attached
- **Build trust** - Demonstrate quality and commitment
- **Convert visitors** - Move people from casual browsers to engaged community members
- **Create entry points** - Multiple paths into teemee.one based on different needs

## Distribution Strategy

### Landing Pages
Each lead magnet gets:
- Dedicated landing page
- Clear value proposition
- Email capture form
- Immediate delivery (email + download link)
- Social proof / testimonials (once available)

### Promotion Channels
- Website homepage
- Blog post CTAs
- Social media
- Newsletter mentions
- Community shares
- Workshop attendee bonuses

## Design Guidelines

### Format
- **PDF preferred** - Professional, printable, shareable
- **Mobile-friendly** - Readable on phones and tablets
- **Print-ready** - If people want physical copies

### Visual Style
- Clean, uncluttered
- Consistent with teemee.one brand
- Professional but warm
- Accessible (good contrast, readable fonts)

### Content Standards
- Immediately actionable
- Example-rich
- Honest and authentic
- No hype or overselling
- Clear next steps

## Email Integration

Every lead magnet triggers:
1. **Immediate delivery email** - Download link + welcome
2. **Day 1 follow-up** - "Did you get a chance to use it?"
3. **Day 3 nurture** - Related prompt or article
4. **Day 7 invitation** - Community or next resource
5. **Weekly newsletter** - Ongoing value

## Metrics

Track for each lead magnet:
- **Downloads** - How many people get it
- **Email capture rate** - % of visitors who sign up
- **Engagement** - Opens, clicks, responses
- **Conversion to next step** - Community joins, workshop signups
- **Quality** - Feedback, testimonials, shares

## Creating New Lead Magnets

Follow this checklist:

- [ ] Aligned with core intentions
- [ ] Solves a specific problem
- [ ] Provides immediate value (not just "learn more")
- [ ] Appropriate length (substantial but not overwhelming)
- [ ] Includes examples and worksheets
- [ ] Clear next steps at the end
- [ ] Professional tone, conversational voice
- [ ] Proofread and tested
- [ ] Landing page created
- [ ] Email sequence written
- [ ] Promotion plan defined

## Notes

**Quality over quantity** - We'd rather have 3 exceptional lead magnets than 10 mediocre ones.

**Evergreen content** - Create resources that stay valuable over time, not trend-dependent.

**Authentic value** - Everything we give away must be genuinely useful. No bait-and-switch, no artificial scarcity.
