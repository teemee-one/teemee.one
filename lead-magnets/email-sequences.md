### Email 1: Prompt Anatomy (Day 0)
**Subject:** The 4 elements every powerful prompt needs

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

Want to know the difference between a mediocre prompt and a transformative one?

It comes down to 4 elements:

**1. CONTEXT** - What's the situation?
"I'm making a career decision..." gives better results than "Help me decide"

**2. INTENTION** - What do you want?
"I want clarity on my values" vs. "Give me advice"

**3. CONSTRAINTS** - What are the boundaries?
"I value honesty over politeness" shapes how AI responds

**4. INVITATION** - What kind of response do you want?
"Ask me questions" vs. "Give me a list" produces different results

**Example:**

❌ Weak: "Help me with my career"

✅ Strong: "I'm considering a job offer that pays 30% more but requires relocation. I have young kids and aging parents nearby. Help me clarify what matters most to me in this decision. Ask me questions to uncover my real priorities, not just my surface concerns."

See the difference?

**Your challenge:** Take any prompt you've used and rewrite it with all 4 elements.
```

### Email 2: The Iteration Principle (Day 2)
**Subject:** Why your first prompt response is never the best one

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

Here's the mistake 90% of people make with prompts:

They accept the first response.

Bad idea.

The real power of AI isn't in the first answer—it's in the **conversation**.

Think of it like therapy: the first session gives you surface insights. The 5th session is where breakthroughs happen.

**How to iterate effectively:**

1. **Read the response** - Don't just skim
2. **Notice what's missing** - What did it not address?
3. **Challenge assumptions** - Where does it feel off?
4. **Go deeper** - Ask "why?" at least 3 times
5. **Test alternatives** - "What if the opposite were true?"

**Example:**

First response: "You should prioritize work-life balance"
Your follow-up: "Why do I assume I need balance? What if integration is better than balance for me?"

This is where magic happens.

**Today's practice:**

Use any prompt. Don't stop at the first response. Go 5 rounds deep.

Tell me what you discover.
```

**Subject:** How to make AI a thinking partner (not just an answer machine)

**Body:**
```
[First Name],

Most people use AI to get answers.

Smart people use AI to **ask better questions**.

Here's the difference:

**Answer-seeking:** "What should I do?"
**Question-seeking:** "What questions should I be asking?"

The second approach makes AI a thinking partner, not just a search engine.

**How to use the Question Method:**

1. Start with your challenge
2. Ask: "What questions would help me think about this more clearly?"
3. Pick the most uncomfortable question
4. Explore that deeply
5. Repeat

**Example:**

Challenge: "I'm unhappy at work"

Questions that emerge:
- "What am I avoiding by staying?"
- "What would I do if money weren't a factor?"
- "What does 'happiness at work' actually mean to me?"
- "Am I trying to fix the job or avoid fixing myself?"

That last one? That's where growth happens.

**Try it:**
```

## 📊 Email Sequence Metrics & Optimization

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | What It Means |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Open Rate** | 35-50% | Subject line effectiveness, list health |
| **Click Rate** | 10-20% | Content relevance, CTA clarity |
| **Reply Rate** | 2-5% | Engagement depth, relationship quality |
| **Conversion Rate** | 3-7% | Workshop signup from sequence |
| **Unsubscribe Rate** | <0.5% | Value delivery, audience fit |

### A/B Testing Ideas

**Subject Lines:**
- Question vs. statement
- Curiosity vs. benefit
- Personal vs. universal
- Emoji vs. plain text

**Content:**
- Story-first vs. lesson-first
- Long-form vs. short-form
- Single CTA vs. multiple options
- Text-only vs. formatted

**Timing:**
- Morning vs. evening
- Weekday vs. weekend
- Immediate vs. delayed follow-up

### Optimization Principles

1. **Respect attention** - Every email should give value before asking for anything
2. **Make unsubscribing easy** - Healthy list > big list
3. **Reply-friendly** - Encourage responses, read and answer them
4. **Personality over polish** - Authentic beats perfect
5. **Test everything** - What works changes over time
