## Overview

teemee.one's promotion has three goals:

1. **Introduce the Vision** - Help people understand what teemee.one is and why it matters
2. **Demonstrate Value** - Show real examples of teemee.one in action
3. **Build Community** - Create conversation and invite participation

## Content Creation Workflow

1. **Plan** - Content calendar decides what to create
2. **Script** - Write or generate scripts
3. **Create** - Make videos, graphics, articles
4. **Review** - Human review for quality and alignment
5. **Publish** - Release across appropriate channels
6. **Measure** - Track metrics and learn
7. **Iterate** - Improve based on what works

## Measurement & Success

**Vanity Metrics (Track But Don't Optimize For)**
- Views, followers, likes
- Good for showing momentum

**Real Metrics (What Actually Matters)**
- People who clone the repo
- Contributors to the project
- Quality of discussions
- People using prompts in real work
- Impact stories

**Launch Success Criteria**
- Website live and showcasing vision
- 5+ pieces of introductory content
- Social media accounts growing
- First 100 clones of the repo
- Positive feedback from early audience

## Brand Voice & Values

All promotional content should:

✓ **Be Authentic** - Show the real teemee.one, not a polished marketing version
✓ **Be Useful** - Every piece should teach something or provide value
✓ **Be Clear** - Explain the vision in accessible terms
✓ **Respect the Audience** - Don't oversell or manipulate
✓ **Align with Intentions** - Create useful tools, foster heart connection, support dreams
