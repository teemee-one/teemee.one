## Contributing

Help grow the knowledge base:

1. **Write about what you know** - Share your insights and learnings
2. **Include metadata** - Title, author, date, tags, summary (see template below)
3. **Cite your sources** - Reference other articles, research, or inspiration
4. **Follow guidelines** - See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Article Template

```markdown
---
title: Article Title
author: Your Name
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [tag1, tag2, tag3]
summary: One-sentence summary of the article
---

Hook and context for the article.

[Detailed explanation and insights]

- [Related article](./link)
```

## Organization Principles

- **Articles** are long-form explorations (1000+ words)
- **Tutorials** are step-by-step how-tos with examples
- **Research** includes data, analysis, charts, and findings
- All content includes metadata for discovery

## Quality Standards

✓ Clear, engaging writing
✓ Well-researched or based on direct experience
✓ Properly formatted and tagged
✓ Useful to the community
✓ Links to related content
✓ Proofread and edited
