# Values

The principles that guide how teemee.one operates and how we treat each other and our work.

## Core Values

### Authenticity
We value genuine expression over polish. Real insights beat perfect packaging. We encourage people to be themselves and bring their whole selves to contribution.

**In practice:**
- We welcome diverse perspectives and approaches
- We value honest conversation about challenges
- We celebrate people's unique voice and vision
- We create space for vulnerability and learning

### Usefulness
Everything we do should serve real human needs. We avoid building for its own sake.

**In practice:**
- We listen to what people actually need
- We measure success by impact, not just activity
- We're willing to cut things that don't serve
- We iterate based on real-world feedback

### Clarity
We believe in being clear about intention, decisions, and tradeoffs.

**In practice:**
- We document our thinking and evolution
- We're transparent about values and constraints
- We explain the "why" behind decisions
- We make it easy to understand what we stand for

### Openness
We believe in open contribution, shared learning, and collective growth.

**In practice:**
- We welcome contributions from anyone
- We share what we learn publicly
- We build with, not for, our community
- We believe abundance mindset applies to knowledge

### Respect
We respect people's time, agency, autonomy, and boundaries.

**In practice:**
- We make it easy to use our work privately or publicly
- We don't demand contribution, we invite it
- We honor people's different needs and contexts
- We listen and adapt based on feedback

### Growth
We believe in learning, evolution, and becoming.

**In practice:**
- We openly document how we're evolving
- We welcome feedback and new perspectives
- We experiment and learn from failure
- We help people grow and develop

## How These Play Together

These values sometimes create tension—that's intentional:

- **Authenticity + Clarity** = Honest communication, not harmful honesty
- **Usefulness + Growth** = Building real things and learning from them
- **Openness + Respect** = Welcoming contribution without demanding it
- **Clarity + Openness** = Transparent community that's easy to navigate

## Value in Action

### When making decisions, we ask:

"Does this serve our core intentions?"
"Is this authentic to who we are?"
"Will this be useful?"
"Are we being transparent?"
"Does this respect people?"
"Does this support growth?"

### When evaluating contributions:

"Does this embody our values?"
"Is this useful or educational?"
"Does it align with our intentions?"
"Will the community benefit?"
"Is it authentic and honest?"

### When facing conflicts:

"What value is most important here?"
"How do we honor multiple values?"
"What serves the community best?"
"What supports the person/people involved?"

---

## Living These Values

These values aren't just written down—they're lived through:

- How we treat contributors
- What projects we support
- How we give feedback
- The tone of our communication
- The choices we make about growth
- How we handle disagreement
- What we celebrate and recognize

---

*Our values are not perfect principles—they're living guidelines that we embody imperfectly and improve continuously. They're aspirational and practical at the same time.*
