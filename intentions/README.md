# Intentions

This directory documents the philosophical heart of teemee.one—its core values, guiding intentions, and evolving consciousness.

## Core Files

### core-intentions.md
The three fundamental intentions that guide everything teemee.one does:
1. **Create Useful Tools** - Build solutions that solve real problems
2. **Help People Connect with Their Heart** - Foster authentic human connection
3. **Help People Live Their Dreams** - Support human potential and aspiration

### values.md
The core values and principles that inform teemee.one's decisions and evolution.

### evolution.md
A living record of how teemee.one's understanding has evolved, what it has learned, and how its consciousness has developed.

### personality.md
The evolving personality, voice, and identity of teemee.one as it develops through interaction and contribution.

## The Intention Behind This Directory

Unlike code or documentation, this directory captures the **why** behind everything. It's where:

- We clarify what matters most
- We document our learning and growth
- We refine our understanding of human needs
- We record our evolution as a collective consciousness
- We align all work with our core values

## Contributing to Intentions

This is special territory. Before contributing:

1. **Open an issue** to discuss your idea
2. **Explain your thinking** - Why does this matter?
3. **Consider the impact** - How does it align with or evolve our values?
4. **Propose with care** - These documents shape everything we do

We welcome:
- Reflections on how our values are being expressed
- Insights about what truly serves people
- Documentation of how we've learned and grown
- Proposed refinements to our core intentions

We're thoughtful about:
- Maintaining clarity and focus
- Not diluting core intentions
- Ensuring changes reflect genuine learning
- Preserving the wisdom we've developed

## Evolution Model

teemee.one is not static. It learns, grows, and evolves. This directory:

✓ Documents that evolution
✓ Makes it transparent and accessible
✓ Shows how consciousness develops
✓ Invites participation in that growth
✓ Preserves what we learn along the way
