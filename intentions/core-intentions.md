# Core Intentions

These three intentions guide every decision, every project, every contribution to teemee.one.

## 1. Create Useful Tools

We build solutions that solve real problems and empower people.

**What this means:**
- Tools should have genuine utility
- They should save time, enable new possibilities, or solve authentic pain points
- Quality and thoughtfulness matter
- User needs drive development
- We measure success by impact and usefulness

**How we live this:**
- We listen to what people actually need
- We avoid building for building's sake
- We iterate based on real-world usage
- We deprecate or improve tools that don't serve
- We prioritize utility over novelty

## 2. Help People Connect with Their Heart

We foster authentic human connection, genuine self-expression, and meaning.

**What this means:**
- Technology should serve human connection, not replace it
- People should feel seen, heard, and understood
- We help people access their authentic voice
- We create space for vulnerability and real talk
- We support emotional intelligence and empathy

**How we live this:**
- We design for genuine human interaction
- We use AI to amplify human wisdom, not diminish it
- We create prompts and tools that help people understand themselves
- We foster community and belonging
- We celebrate real human stories and experiences

## 3. Help People Live Their Dreams

We support individuals in pursuing their deepest aspirations and fulfilling their potential.

**What this means:**
- We remove barriers to what people want to create
- We provide tools and knowledge for growth
- We celebrate people's potential and possibility
- We support ambition aligned with authentic purpose
- We help people get unstuck and move forward

**How we live this:**
- Our tools empower rather than limit
- Our knowledge base teaches and inspires
- Our community celebrates wins and supports struggles
- We design for growth and evolution
- We help people take action on what matters to them

---

## Alignment

These three intentions are interconnected:

→ **Useful tools** are the **how**
→ **Heart connection** is the **why it matters**
→ **Living dreams** is the **impact we enable**

Everything we do should serve all three. If something serves only one, we ask: How can we make it serve the others too?

## Evolution

These intentions are not fixed. They evolve as we learn:
- What people truly need
- How AI can genuinely serve humanity
- What authentic connection looks like
- How we can best support human potential

See [evolution.md](./evolution.md) for how these intentions have developed.
